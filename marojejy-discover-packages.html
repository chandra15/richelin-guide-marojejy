Marojejy Discover

Discover the incredible rainforest, wildlife and landscapes of Marojejy National Park on this unforgettable two-day adventure.

Duration 2 Days / 1 Night
Difficulty Moderate
Location Marojejy National Park
Best Season Year-round

Experience the Magic of Marojejy

The Marojejy Discover package is an introduction to one of Madagascar's most remarkable natural environments. Over two days and one night, explore pristine rainforest trails, discover endemic wildlife and experience the atmosphere of Marojejy National Park.

Accompanied by local guides, you will explore the forest, observe unique plants and wildlife, and enjoy an authentic experience surrounded by the spectacular landscapes of northern Madagascar.

Your Journey

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Your adventure begins at the entrance of Marojejy National Park in Manantenina. After meeting your local guide and porter team, begin your trek through the dense rainforest towards Camp Marojejy.

Along the trail, discover endemic plants, birds, chameleons and other wildlife. After arriving at camp, enjoy dinner before heading out on a nocturnal walk to discover the forest after dark.

  • Meals: Lunch, Dinner
  • Accommodation: Camp Marojejy

Wake up to the sounds of the rainforest and enjoy breakfast before setting out on a morning wildlife walk.

After exploring the surrounding forest and taking in the mountain scenery, return to camp before beginning your descent towards Manantenina.

  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch
  • End of Tour: Afternoon arrival at Manantenina

What's Included

  • National park entrance fees & permits
  • Professional English/French guide
  • Local camp porters & cook
  • All meals during the tour
  • 1-night camp accommodation

What's Excluded

  • Transport to/from Andapa/Manantenina
  • Personal hiking gear & sleeping bag
  • Alcoholic beverages & bottled water
  • Tips and gratuities
