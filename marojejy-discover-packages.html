Marojejy Discover

Discover the incredible rainforest, wildlife and landscapes of Marojejy National Park on this unforgettable two-day adventure.

Duration 2 Days / 1 Night
Price €898 / Person
Location Marojejy National Park
Transport Sambava to Last Village

Package price is per person. Park fees may vary depending on the month and season, so please confirm the final price for your travel date.

Experience the Magic of Marojejy

The Marojejy Discover package offers an authentic experience in the rainforest of Marojejy National Park. Over two days and one night, discover the natural beauty of the park while travelling with a local guide and support team.

Transportation from Sambava to the last village is included. During the trek, food and supplies are carried to the camp by porters, as food is purchased in Sambava before entering the forest.

Important Information

Trekking gear is not provided with the package. Guests should bring their own suitable trekking equipment.

Food is purchased in Sambava before the trek and transported to the camp by porters. There is no food available in the forest.

National park fees are included in the package but may vary according to the month and season.

Your Journey

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Begin your journey from Sambava, with transportation arranged to the last village before entering the Marojejy National Park area.

Meet your local guide and support team before beginning the trek through the rainforest towards the bungalow camp.

  • Transport: Sambava to the last village
  • Food: Supplies purchased in Sambava
  • Trek: Forest trek towards camp
  • Accommodation: Bungalow

Continue exploring the rainforest with your local guide and discover the unique wildlife and natural surroundings of Marojejy.

After completing the day's activities, begin your return journey from the forest towards the last village.

  • Experience: Wildlife and rainforest exploration
  • Guide: Local trekking guide
  • Meals: Food included
  • Return: Descent towards the last village

What's Included

  • National park fees & tickets
  • Local guide
  • Special guide for Silky Sifaka
  • Bungalow accommodation
  • Cook
  • Porter
  • Kitchen use
  • Food & meals
  • Transportation from Sambava to the last village

What's Excluded

  • Personal trekking gear
  • Personal expenses
  • Tips and gratuities
